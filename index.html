How it works

1. Register once with your details and photo — you'll get a unique member number.

2. Pay the KSh 120 registration fee to 0118665120, then paste your M-Pesa confirmation message on the Pay page. An admin reviews and activates you.

3. Each new semester, pay the KSh 50 subscription the same way to stay active.

4. Join welfare drives — like maize collections — by logging your contribution with your member number.
